For this project, I explored a student mental health dataset from a Japanese international university using PostgreSQL.

The main goal was to understand how depression, social connectedness, and acculturative stress vary among international students, and whether the length of stay in Japan has any relationship with these factors.

- What I Explored

I focused on three main mental health indicators:

1. Depression (PHQ-9): Measures the severity of depressive symptoms.

2. Social Connectedness (SCS): Measures how connected students feel to others.

3. Acculturative Stress (ASISS): Measures stress associated with adapting to a new culture.

I also looked at how these scores varied depending on how long students had stayed in Japan.

- Tools Used

1. SQL

- What I Did

Using SQL, I:

1. Explored the dataset and its different columns.

2. Filtered the data to focus on international students.

3. Found the minimum and maximum depression scores.

4. Grouped students by their length of stay.

5. Calculated the average depression, social connectedness, and acculturative stress scores for each group.

6. Used aggregate functions to summarise and compare the results.
